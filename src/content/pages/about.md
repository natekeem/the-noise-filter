The Noise Filter explains widely discussed product, platform, subscription, safety, and culture changes for adults who want the point without the noise.

This is an independent explainer publication concept, not a newsroom. Articles are based on clearly identified sources, with official or primary sources used whenever available. The site does not claim first-hand reporting, private access, legal advice, financial advice, or product testing unless a future article can support that claim directly.

Each article follows the same basic promise: what happened, why it matters, what is confirmed, what remains unclear, what regular users should check, and what to watch next.

This project is an MVP. The name and domain have not been legally cleared, no articles are public, and every draft requires source review and explicit human approval before publication.
