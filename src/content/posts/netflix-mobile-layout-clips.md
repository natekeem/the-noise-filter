If Netflix suddenly looks different on your phone, you did not open the wrong app. Netflix has refreshed its mobile experience and added a vertical discovery feed called Clips. The layout and the feed do not reach every device or country in exactly the same way, so two people with updated Netflix apps may still see different screens.

The useful part of this change is not the promise that discovery will be more fun. It is knowing where familiar controls moved, what the Clips feed can do, and why a feature shown in screenshots may not be available in your country yet.

## What happened

Netflix announced the refreshed mobile design on April 30, 2026. The company initially listed the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, India, Malaysia, Pakistan, the Philippines, and South Africa among the launch markets. On July 14, Netflix updated that announcement to say the design was also available in Brazil, France, Germany, Korea, and Mexico.

The refreshed layout applies to Netflix on Android phones and tablets, iPhone, and iPad with the latest app. Netflix's Help Center describes a bottom navigation bar with Home, Clips where available, Search, and My Netflix. Filters and shortcuts for shows, movies, games, podcasts, New & Hot, and categories sit near the top of the screen.

Clips is a separate part of the rollout. It is currently available in select countries on Android phones and iPhones with the latest app. It presents a continuous vertical feed of short clips from shows, movies, and podcasts. From a clip, a user can open the full title, add it to My List, or share it.

## Why it matters

The most visible change is the Clips tab. Netflix describes it as a way to discover something to watch, listen to, or play next. It is not a place for user-generated posts. The Help Center says the current feed includes clips from popular shows, movies, and podcasts.

The redesign also moves familiar destinations. Netflix's help page says New & Hot is now in the shortcuts near the top of the screen, and Games can appear there or in a Mobile Games row. A person looking for an old bottom-tab shortcut may reasonably think it disappeared.

Netflix has also said it plans to expand Clips with live programming and genre- or interest-based collections. Those are future-facing plans, not a checklist of features that every subscriber has now.

## What is confirmed

- The refreshed layout supports Android phones and tablets, iPhone, and iPad with the latest Netflix app.
- The bottom navigation has been reorganized, while New & Hot and Games remain available through top shortcuts or, for Games, a Mobile Games row.
- Clips lets users watch short clips, open the whole title, add a title to My List, or share it.
- Clips is currently available in select countries on Android phones and iPhones, not all countries or all mobile device types.
- Netflix updated its April announcement on July 14 to include Brazil, France, Germany, Korea, and Mexico in the announced availability list.

## What is still unclear

Netflix has not published one permanent, exhaustive country-by-country timetable for the rest of the rollout. Its Help Center says Clips will expand globally over the next several months, but that is a broad window rather than a date for each market.

The sources do not give a personal rollout date for every account. Netflix confirms that the latest app is required, but an updated app does not guarantee that Clips is already enabled in every country.

Netflix has described future additions such as live programming, podcasts, and themed collections. Those plans may change, and readers should not assume that every content type is already present in their Clips feed.

## What regular users should check

1. **Update the Netflix app.** Use the official Apple App Store or Google Play listing.
2. **Look at the top shortcuts.** If New & Hot or Games is missing from the bottom bar, swipe through the shortcuts near the top of the home screen.
3. **Do not treat a missing Clips tab as an account problem.** Netflix says the feature is not available in all countries yet.
4. **Check the title before acting on a clip.** Clips are a discovery tool. Opening the title page is the simplest way to confirm whether it is a film, series, podcast, game, or something else available with your account.
5. **Use My List if you do not want to decide immediately.** Clips includes an add-to-list action, so there is no need to start a title just to save it.

The practical rule is simple: a changed navigation bar is part of the redesign; a missing Clips feed may just mean the staged rollout has not reached your market.

## What to watch next

Watch Netflix's Help Center for changes to country availability and supported devices. Also watch whether live programming and themed collections move from announced plans into the version of Clips that ordinary subscribers can actually use.
