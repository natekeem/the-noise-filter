If Netflix suddenly looks different on your phone, you did not open the wrong app. Netflix has redesigned its mobile experience and added a short-video discovery feed called Clips. The layout is available more broadly than Clips itself, so two people with updated Netflix apps may still see different screens.

The useful part of this change is not the promise that discovery will be more fun. It is knowing where familiar controls moved, what the Clips feed can do, and why a feature shown in screenshots may not be available in your country yet.

## What happened

Netflix announced the refreshed mobile design on April 30, 2026. The company initially listed the United States, United Kingdom, Australia, Canada, India, Malaysia, Pakistan, the Philippines, and South Africa among the launch markets. On July 14, Netflix updated the announcement to add Brazil, France, Germany, Korea, and Mexico.

The new layout applies to Netflix on Android phones and tablets, iPhone, and iPad when the app is up to date. Netflix's help page describes a bottom navigation bar with Home, Search, My Netflix, and, in supported countries, Clips. Filters and shortcuts for items such as shows, movies, games, podcasts, New & Hot, and categories sit near the top of the screen.

Clips is a separate part of the rollout. It presents a continuous vertical feed of short excerpts from Netflix titles. From a clip, a user can open the full title, add it to My List, or share it.

## Why it matters

The most visible change is the Clips tab. It looks familiar to anyone who has used a vertical short-video feed, but Netflix says its purpose is title discovery rather than user-generated posting. The clips come from shows, movies, specials, and, in some versions of the company's description, podcasts.

The redesign also moves familiar destinations. Netflix's help page says New & Hot is now in the shortcuts near the top of the screen, and Games can appear there or in a Mobile Games row. A person looking for an old bottom-tab shortcut may reasonably think it disappeared.

There is also a larger strategy behind the interface. Netflix says it plans to expand Clips over time to include more formats and themed collections. That future direction is worth watching, but it should not be confused with what every subscriber has today.

## What is confirmed

- The refreshed layout supports Android phones and tablets, iPhone, and iPad with the latest Netflix app.
- The bottom navigation has been reorganized, while New & Hot and Games remain available through other locations in the interface.
- Clips lets users browse short excerpts, open the full title, add a title to My List, or share it.
- Clips is available only in selected countries for now.
- Netflix added Brazil, France, Germany, Korea, and Mexico to the announced availability list on July 14, 2026.

## What is still unclear

Netflix has not published one permanent, exhaustive country-by-country timetable for the rest of the rollout. Its help page says Clips will expand globally over the next several months, but that is a broad window rather than a date for each market.

It is also possible for rollout status to vary by device, operating system, app version, account, or staged release timing. Netflix confirms that the latest app is required, but an updated app does not guarantee that Clips is already enabled in every country.

Netflix has described future additions such as live programming, podcasts, and themed collections. Those plans may change, and readers should not assume that every content type is already present in their Clips feed.

## What regular users should check

1. **Update the Netflix app.** Use the official Apple App Store or Google Play listing rather than an update link in an email or text message.
2. **Look at the top shortcuts.** If New & Hot or Games is missing from the bottom bar, swipe through the shortcuts near the top of the home screen.
3. **Do not treat a missing Clips tab as an account problem.** Netflix says the feature is not available in all countries yet.
4. **Check the title before acting on a clip.** Clips are a discovery tool. Opening the title page is the simplest way to confirm whether it is a film, series, podcast, game, or something else available with your account.
5. **Use My List if you do not want to decide immediately.** The feed includes an add-to-list action, so there is no need to start a title just to save it.

The practical rule is simple: a changed navigation bar is part of the redesign; a missing Clips feed may just mean the staged rollout has not reached your market.

## What to watch next

Watch Netflix's help page for changes to country availability and supported devices. Also watch whether podcasts, live programming, and themed collections move from announced plans into the version of Clips that ordinary subscribers can actually use.

For the site itself, the important question is whether Netflix keeps Clips as an optional discovery surface or makes it a central route through the mobile app. That will matter more to regular users than the visual resemblance to other short-video products.
